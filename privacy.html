Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data Collection

When you use MyCVKH, we collect the personal information you enter into the form fields (such as your name, contact details, work history, and education) solely for the purpose of generating your CV [web:15].

2. Data Usage & Storage

Your CV data is processed locally in your browser and temporarily on our secure servers to generate the PDF file. We do not store your personal CV data long-term. Once your PDF is generated and your session ends, the data entered into the builder is discarded.

3. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We respect your privacy. MyCVKH does not sell, rent, or share your personal information, email address, or phone number with recruiters, third-party marketing agencies, or advertisers [web:15][web:17].

4. Payment Information

If you pay via KHQR or our integrated payment gateway, your payment data is processed securely by the bank. MyCVKH does not view, collect, or store your banking credentials or full credit card numbers.

5. Local Storage (Auto-Save)

Our website may use your browser's Local Storage to temporarily save your form progress so you don't lose your work if you accidentally refresh the page. This data remains on your personal device, not our servers.
